# LW-146: Explain interrupted staging before its removal

Status: planned. Minted 2026-10-25 by the research programme (docs/51 section 13).
Milestone: M3. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-040, LW-041.
Owner decision HUMAN_TODO q-47 Q1 (2026-10-25): staging removal is allowed, "but it should assess whether there is something that can be done and help the user understand what happened and index or give more info if needed before deleting".

## Scope
Before copy-and-verify removes its own unpublished staging after a cancel or abandon, Loomward explains the run: why it stopped (the journaled refusal or cancel), which items were verified and which were not, whether a resume is possible, and what evidence will remain afterwards (docs/49 section 4, docs/48 section 9). It writes a bounded disposition record (counts, refusal codes, verified and unverified item counts, staging identity) to the journal first, and only then performs the existing identity-bound removal. `LOOMWARD_COPY_STAGING_REMOVAL` (`crates/loomward-service/src/copy.rs`) stays false until this lands and is reviewed.

## Acceptance evidence
Lab fixtures for each stop: a resumable refusal and a terminal one get different explanations; no removal when the staging identity or journal membership is uncertain; a published copy is never removed; failure to persist the disposition record leaves staging intact; the explanation needs no model (deterministic templates over journal facts; a docs/49 summary is optional extra text); crash points between the record and the removal leave either staging intact or removed by identity, never half-removed without a record.
