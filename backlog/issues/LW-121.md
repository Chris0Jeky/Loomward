# LW-121: Add growth and low-space monitoring with alerts

Status: planned. Minted from the owner decisions of 2026-10-12.
Milestone: M6. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-008, LW-051, LW-107.
Owner decision HUMAN_TODO q-34 (2026-10-12).

## Scope
Alerts for fast-growing folders and low free space on granted volumes, driven by the watchers and read-only telemetry. Local notifications only, off until the owner enables them, with coverage and uncertainty stated. Alerts are advice, not actions.

## Acceptance evidence
Synthetic growth and low-space fixtures raise exactly the expected alerts; coverage gaps suppress or qualify an alert rather than reading as zero; alert thresholds are user settings with documented defaults; no network and no background service beyond the existing app.

## Notes
Cross-reference: LW-060 (growth and anomaly forecasts) and LW-051 (resource trends).

## Boundary
No additional capability is implicitly authorised by this issue. Keep file and process mutations, elevation, cloud
transfer of personal data and public release off unless the decision above and a named safety gate say otherwise.
Synthetic data only in Git.
