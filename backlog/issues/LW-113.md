# LW-113: Build the live full-text and substring search index over granted roots

Status: in_progress. Minted from the owner decisions of 2026-10-12.
Milestone: M2. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-005, LW-008.
Owner decision HUMAN_TODO q-34 (2026-10-12); Owner decision HUMAN_TODO q-36 (2026-10-12).

## Scope
Find & Ask backbone: Loomward's own full-text and substring index over granted roots, kept live by the non-elevated watchers (overflow handled by the LW-008 reconciliation). This item delivers the base index and the owner-local query endpoint. Names and paths first; content terms remain disabled until the sandboxed extraction worker (LW-014) is separately qualified and enabled for the applicable grant.

## Shared-query gate (follow-up to #422 and #429)
LW-005 and LW-008 are sufficient for the owner-local index only. Completing this item does not expose owner-wide results to a model or external agent. The source-of-truth manifest schedules **LW-113 -> LW-069 -> LW-066/LW-118**: LW-069 waits for this index and LW-067's client grants, owns the lexical shared adapter, and cannot complete before its grant canaries pass. The MCP and Ask consumers wait for that adapter. LW-058 remains the earlier API foundation, not authority to register this new shared search surface.

The shared adapter must reject an ungranted client, filter paths/names/content fields and derived totals to the client's grant, bind cursors and cached results to identity and grant epoch, reject cross-client cursor reuse, and recheck authority when explaining a listed result. Revocation or forget invalidates outstanding pages and citations. Content extraction and cloud disclosure retain separate consent gates.

The optional Everything backend is qualified separately under LW-114 after the shared interface exists. The lexical adapter and its consumers do not depend on Everything, and this item never installs it or scans a whole disk implicitly.

## Acceptance evidence
Query latency and index-size measurements on the synthetic scale lab, recorded in an evidence file; owner-local results never outlive grant revocation or forget; live updates survive watcher overflow; partial/stale coverage stays visible rather than becoming zero results; no elevation or whole-disk scan. The completion receipt identifies the endpoint as owner-local and reports shared MCP/Ask exposure as gated until LW-069 qualifies it.

LW-069's separate acceptance cases include an ungranted client, a one-root client against a multi-root index, excluded totals and content fields, cross-client cursor reuse, and revocation between list and explanation. This planning document does not claim those runtime tests passed.
