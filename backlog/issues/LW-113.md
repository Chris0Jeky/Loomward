# LW-113: Build the live full-text and substring search index over granted roots

Status: in_progress. Minted from the owner decisions of 2026-10-12.
Milestone: M2. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-005, LW-008.
Owner decision HUMAN_TODO q-34 (2026-10-12); Owner decision HUMAN_TODO q-36 (2026-10-12).

## Scope
Find & Ask backbone: Loomward's own full-text and substring index over granted roots, kept live by the non-elevated watchers (overflow handled by the LW-008 reconciliation). This item delivers the base index and the owner-local query endpoint. Names and paths first; content terms remain disabled until the sandboxed extraction worker (LW-014) is separately qualified and enabled for the applicable grant.

## Shared-query gate (follow-up to #422)
LW-005 and LW-008 are sufficient dependencies only for the owner-local index. Completing this item does not make its global owner results available to a model or an external agent. The shared projection belongs to LW-117/LW-118 and the external-agent API LW-058, and cannot be exposed until LW-067's per-client grants, field projections and revocation epochs are implemented and their canaries pass. Code reuse does not transfer owner authority to an MCP client or an Ask provider. This split applies to the ordinary lexical index as well as LW-114's optional Everything adapter.

The shared surface must deny a client without a grant; filter paths, names, content terms and all derived totals to the client's roots and disclosure fields; bind cursors and cached results to client identity and grant epoch; reject cross-client cursor reuse; and recheck authority when explaining or resolving a listed result. Revocation or forget invalidates outstanding pages and citations. Content extraction and cloud disclosure retain their separate owner-consent gates.

## Acceptance evidence
Query latency and index-size measurements on the synthetic scale lab, recorded in an evidence file; owner-local results never outlive a grant revocation or forget; live updates survive watcher overflow (fixture); partial or stale coverage is shown, never replaced by zero results; no whole-disk scan and no elevation. The completion receipt must identify the endpoint as owner-local and explicitly report shared MCP/Ask exposure as gated, not implemented by this item.

Before the dependent shared-query implementation is enabled, its tests must cover an ungranted client, a one-root client against a multi-root index, excluded totals and content fields, cross-client cursor reuse, and revocation between list and explanation. These are required downstream acceptance cases, not passing evidence supplied by this planning document.
