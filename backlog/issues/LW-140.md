# LW-140: Small-model tournament for categorisation and summaries

Status: planned. Minted 2026-10-25 by the research programme (docs/51).
Milestone: M2. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-138, LW-117.
Owner question Q4 (docs/51 section 9) is open: approve about 20 to 25 GB of model downloads to `E:` through LM Studio, and name the 27B baseline model, revision and quantisation the owner runs today (not yet recorded).

## Scope
Run the small-model tournament for the categoriser's uncertain cases and for one-line summaries (docs/51 section 4.8, stage 3; ADR R4). Candidates are Laya for typed decisions and 0.8 to 4B generative models for the sentence; a Q4 to Q8 quantisation sweep runs on the best candidates. The runner reads each model card and records the digest of every downloaded file (docs/51 section 2.7). Models are reached through the OpenAI-compatible provider interface (LW-117), for example llama.cpp or LM Studio. Model release facts in the report are unverified until this item reads them (docs/51 section 3 item 5).

## Acceptance evidence
The promotion bar of docs/51 section 2.4 applies to the uncertain subset: a candidate must reach 97% of the 27B baseline's macro-F1, be faster, produce 99.5% complete structured output and make no unsupported claims; otherwise nothing is promoted (docs/51 section 5 P3). Benchmarks use group and time splits and an out-of-distribution set of new projects and shoots; no vendor benchmark substitutes for the owner's corpus. Every claim in a generated summary traces to a packet fact. Each model's source, licence and file digests are recorded before use.
