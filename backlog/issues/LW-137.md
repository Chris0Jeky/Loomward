# LW-137: Storage-path identity and volume health as domain objects

Status: planned. Minted 2026-10-25 by the research programme (docs/51).
Milestone: M4. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-004.
Owner decisions HUMAN_TODO q-47 and q-48 (2026-10-25) bind this programme (docs/51 header).

## Scope
Make the storage path and volume health domain objects, never the drive letter (docs/51 section 4.10, ADR R6). A `StoragePath` record per volume holds the volume GUID, disk model and firmware, bus type, USB VID/PID and bridge where discoverable, BOT or UAS, logical and physical sector size, removal policy, write-cache state and the Windows build; the serial number stays out of Git. A `HealthObservation` holds temperature, errors, power-on hours, wear and start/stop counts where `MSFT_StorageReliabilityCounter` reports them. The drive letter is presentation only. It builds on the volume identity and capability inventory already done (LW-004). It starts after the external-disk lane's E0 lands, because `crates/loomward-windows/src/volumes.rs` is owned by that lane this wave (docs/51 sections 4.10 and 12).

## Acceptance evidence
An empty health result is `unsupported_or_hidden` and is never shown as healthy; SMART behind a USB bridge shows unknown (docs/51 section 2.7). Tests use a synthetic storage-query provider with three fixtures: reliability counters present, an empty result, and a bridge that hides the counters. A drive-letter change keeps the storage-path identity; a changed enclosure, firmware or removal policy changes the tuple. Sector geometry (512e or 4Kn) from the existing alignment read is recorded per path. Removal policy and write-cache state are recorded as observed values, and unknown when the read fails.
