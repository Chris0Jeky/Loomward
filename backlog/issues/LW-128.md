# LW-128: Nice-to-have: paired machines with a read-only LAN view

Status: planned. Minted from the owner decisions of 2026-10-12.
Milestone: MX. Priority: P2.

## Scope
View another paired machine's summaries read-only over the LAN, for example this PC and Kraspyon. Explicit pairing, authenticated per-user transport, scoped disclosure grants; no remote effects. Optional, after local-only work.

## Acceptance evidence
Threat model and pairing design reviewed; a two-instance loopback prototype proving read-only scope and revocation; nothing is exposed by default.
