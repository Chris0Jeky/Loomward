# LW-139: Classical student ensemble with labelling functions

Status: planned. Minted 2026-10-25 by the research programme (docs/51).
Milestone: M2. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-138, LW-025.
Owner decisions HUMAN_TODO q-47 and q-48 (2026-10-25) bind this programme (docs/51 header).

## Scope
Add the classical students and the labelling functions under the categoriser cascade (docs/51 section 4.8, stage 2). Labelling functions are rules that vote or abstain on packet facts; for example `Cargo.toml` plus a dominant `target/` votes `generated_build`, and a `.lrcat` plus a dated `Backups/` folder votes `catalogue_backup`. A hashed path n-gram linear model and a structural tree model over packet numbers feed a weak-supervision label model that combines them with the votes. Every output can abstain. The tiny embedding prototypes belong to LW-024, not this item. The LLM teacher stays a separate, confined labelling source (invariant 3; LW-111).

## Acceptance evidence
Each labelling function has fixtures for its vote and for its abstention. The combined output abstains where the votes conflict rather than guessing. The grouped temporal split, macro-F1 and selective risk at coverage 0.5, 0.8, 0.9 and 0.98 are reported with the LW-025 machinery (docs/51 section 5 P2). Calibration on held-out owner labels waits for X4 and is not claimed from synthetic data. Teacher labels are stored as their own source class and are never mixed with owner labels (invariant 3).
