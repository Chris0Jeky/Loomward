**Status:** planned continuation; the v0.2 reference is supporting evidence, not completion of this production issue.

## Outcome and boundaries

Validate versioned provider descriptors, requested capabilities, settings schema and declared provenance. Store unconfigured, validating, ready, degraded, blocked and revoked states separately. Installation and connection require a distinct owner action.
