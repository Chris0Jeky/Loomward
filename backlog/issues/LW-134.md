# LW-134: Physical power-loss and unplug qualification on disposable media

Status: planned. Minted 2026-10-25 by the research programme (docs/51).
Milestone: M3. Priority: P2.
Dependencies: LW-133, LW-137.
Owner question Q3 (docs/51 section 9) is open: (a) defer, recommended for now, or (b) buy a disposable disk in the same enclosure model plus a programmable USB power switch. Nothing here starts on an inferred answer.

## Scope
Qualify the power-loss claim on a real storage path: physical power removal and unplug campaigns on disposable media behind the same USB bridge and firmware as the storage-path tuple of LW-137 (docs/51 sections 4.1 and 10, X9). Its evidence attaches to that tuple, which is what lets a receipt say "power-loss qualified (this storage path)" (ADR R6); a different enclosure invalidates it. Hyper-V (LW-133) cannot prove that a bridge or HDD honours a flush when its own power goes, so this item is the only route to that label. Until it lands, copies to USB paths say "not power-loss tested on this disk" (docs/51 section 4.1). The item runs only if the owner picks option (b) and buys the hardware.

## Acceptance evidence
A power-cut and unplug campaign on the disposable disk, with each cut's outcome attached to the recorded tuple: VID/PID, bridge, firmware, disk model, Windows build, removal policy, write-cache state and sector geometry (LW-137). Zero invariant violations at the copy-engine crash points. An unplug during a copy either resumes or refuses, and never produces a false receipt. Receipt labels follow the result: "power-loss qualified" only for the qualified tuple, and the "not power-loss tested on this disk" copy otherwise (docs/51 section 4.1). A changed enclosure, firmware or removal policy voids the qualification (R6). Lab data only.

## Notes
Duplicate check: LW-133 (Hyper-V guest hard power-offs) is the nearest and is explicitly not a substitute, because a guest loses only its own caches. LW-137 owns the storage-path tuple this evidence attaches to, and LW-041 owns the crash matrix whose points the campaign reuses. This item is distinct as real device power removal and unplug on hardware. A custom storage minifilter for crash ordering is deferred and not part of this item (docs/51 section 6).
