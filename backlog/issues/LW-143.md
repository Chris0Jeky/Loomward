# LW-143: Photo working sets and a prioritised transfer queue

Status: planned. Minted 2026-10-25 by the research programme (docs/51).
Milestone: M6. Priority: P1.
Dependencies: LW-124, LW-142.
Owner decisions HUMAN_TODO q-47 and q-48 (2026-10-25) bind this programme (docs/51 header).

## Scope
Add explicit photo working sets and a prioritised transfer queue (docs/51 sections 2.5, 4.9 and 5 P5; ADR R5). Lightroom gets three modes: archive in place (the default), a copy into a working set, or a Lightroom-managed move. A locked `.lrcat` refuses any effect, and a RAW with its XMP travels as one sidecar bundle. The queue has priorities P0 to P4 and runs over the verified-copy primitive; personal data moves only once the copy gate of LW-124 is in force. CfAPI and ProjFS placeholders are not part of this item (deferred, docs/51 section 6).

## Acceptance evidence
A locked `.lrcat` refuses every effect with the existing `source_in_use` refusal. A RAW and its XMP sidecar form one bundle in every plan. The queue orders P0 to P4 and resumes after an unplug without repeating items already verified (docs/51 section 5 P5). No personal-data transfer runs before LW-124's gate. Working-set membership is explicit and listed, never inferred from the preview cache. Tests use disposable synthetic folders; the owner's own RAW folders are used only in the X7 benchmark.

## Notes
Duplicate check: LW-124 (the approved copy-and-verify gate) and LW-142 (the preview catalogue) are the nearest. This item adds the working sets and the transfer queue over LW-124's verified-copy primitive, which neither owns. The queue does not create a second copy path.
