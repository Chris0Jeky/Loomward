covers the code and documents; it does not grant any right to the name beyond what this page says.

## You may

- Redistribute **unmodified** copies of Loomward under the name Loomward.
- Describe your work factually, for example "based on Loomward", "a fork of Loomward" or "works with
  Loomward".
- Refer to the project by name in articles, reviews, issues and discussions.

## You may not

- Use "Loomward", or a name or logo easily confused with it, for a **modified version or a fork** you
  distribute. Pick a different name for it.
- Say or imply that your product, service or modified version is made, endorsed, certified or
  supported by the Loomward project or its maintainer.

A change is "modified" if it alters the source, the build or the behaviour of what you distribute.
Packaging unmodified upstream releases is not a modification.
